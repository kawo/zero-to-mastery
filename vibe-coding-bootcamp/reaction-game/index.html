    How it works

    1. Press Start session or tap the pad.
    2. The shape turns amber. A random 1–5 s delay is running, so don’t guess.
    3. When it turns green, click, tap or press Space.
    4. Reacting early, or in under 100 ms, counts as a false start and isn’t averaged.
    5. Beat the level’s target 3 times in a row to level up. Higher levels add blue decoys and drop the text cue. Two misses in a row drops you a level.
    6. Rounds under the target can drop power-ups (Shield, Double, Leeway, Calm). Use them between rounds with 1–3. They help you level up but never change your times.
    7. Playing with friends? Tournament lets 2–8 players take turns on this device and ranks everyone at the end.
