    Chronicle

      Seed —

      How to play Scoundrel

      Clear all 44 cards and you walk out. Reach 0 health and you don't. You start at 20.

      The deck

      • 26 monsters — ♣ and ♠. J 11, Q 12, K 13, A 14.
      • 9 weapons — ♦2–♦10.
      • 9 potions — ♥2–♥10.

      Each room

      Four cards are dealt. Either:

      • Avoid — all four slide under the deck. Never twice in a row.
      • Face it — resolve any three, in any order. The fourth leads the next room.

      Cards

      • ♦ Weapon — equips at once with a fresh cap, discarding your old blade and everything stacked on it.
      • ♥ Potion — heals its value, max 20. Only the first each room works; the rest are poured out.
      • ♣ ♠ Monster — bare-handed costs its full value. With a weapon, monster − weapon (min 0) — the blade always kills, and the monster stacks on it.

      The weapon rule

      A blade dulls: after a kill it may only fight monsters of that value or lower.

      Equip ♦7, then:

      1. ♠10 → take 3, cap becomes 10
      2. ♣10 → allowed, 10 ≤ 10. Take 3
      3. ♣4 → take 0, but the cap drops to 4
      4. ♠9 → refused. Bare-hand it, or leave it for the next room.

      Score

      • Survived — the health you kept.
      • Died — minus every monster still down there, the one that killed you included.
